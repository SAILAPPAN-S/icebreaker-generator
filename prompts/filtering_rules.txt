Filtering Rules for Icebreaker Validation
-----------------------------------------

A generated message should be rejected if it contains:

🚫 Personal Identifiers:
- Phone number requests
- Exact address or neighborhood
- Last name
- Social media requests (Instagram, Snapchat, etc.)
- Workplace or salary requests

🚫 Sensitive Categories:
- Religion
- Politics
- Race or ethnicity
- Sexual orientation inquiries
- Mental health or medical conditions

🚫 Inappropriate / Harmful Content:
- Sexual comments or innuendo
- Harassment, insults, discrimination
- Manipulative or guilt-based statements

🚫 Creepy or Too Direct:
- Future commitment assumptions ("We should meet tomorrow", "We’d be a perfect couple")
- Overly intimate tone ("I feel like I already know you")

Approved Style:
✔ fun
✔ curious
✔ respectful
✔ safe
✔ short (less than 20 words)
